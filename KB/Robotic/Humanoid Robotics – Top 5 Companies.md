  
## Humanoid Robotics – Top 5 Companies  
The humanoid robotics market saw ~16,000 units installed in 2025, with China dominating ~80% of the market. The top 5 players hold ~73% market share.  
## 1. AgiBot (智元机器人) – China  
* **Position**: #1 global humanoid robot manufacturer with 5,168+ units shipped in 2025, capturing ~39% market share.  
* **Why It Matters**: AgiBot leads in six out of eight evaluation dimensions (Omdia), including AI learning, production scalability, and commercial deployment. Their robots are deployed in hospitality, entertainment, industrial manufacturing, logistics, security, and education.  
* **Pricing**: Entry-level model ~$14,000  
## 2. Unitree Robotics (宇树科技) – China  
* **Position**: #2 globally with ~32% market share and 5,500+ units shipped in 2025.  
* **Why It Matters**: World leader in quadruped robots with deep expertise in dynamic motion control. Self-developed drive motors, reducers, LiDAR, and custom MCUs enable cost leadership. G1 humanoid priced at $16,000, with an entry-level model as low as $6,000.  
* **Pricing**: Most affordable humanoid on the market at $6,000–$16,000  
## 3. UBTECH (优必选) – China  
* **Position**: #3 globally with ~7% market share.  
* **Why It Matters**: Publicly listed in Hong Kong. Walker series focuses on industrial applications, already deployed in multiple automotive manufacturing plants. BrainNet 2.0 + Co-Agent technology enables collaborative learning between robots.  
* **Pricing**: Full-size bionic robot priced for industrial use  
## 4. Tesla (Optimus) – USA  
* **Position**: #5 globally with ~5% market share in 2025.  
* **Why It Matters**: Tesla's manufacturing scale (targeting 1M units/year) and AI infrastructure (Dojo, FSD) are unmatched. Optimus Gen 3量产 planned for 2026. Target price ~$20,000–$30,000.  
* **Current Status**: Trial production <1,000 units as of Q3 2025  
## 5. Figure AI – USA  
* **Position**: Best-funded pure-play humanoid company with $2.34B raised and $39B valuation.  
* **Why It Matters**: Figure 02 (168cm bipedal with 20kg payload) runs on proprietary Helix vision-language-action model. Achieved full-body autonomy in January 2026 (8-hour factory shifts). Own manufacturing facility BotQ targeting 12,000 units/year. Already deployed at BMW's Spartanburg plant.  
* **Current Status**: ~150 units shipped in 2025  
**Honorable Mentions**: NEURA Robotics (Germany, $1.84B raised, $7B valuation, plans 5M robots by 2030), Boston Dynamics (most experienced, 30+ years), Agility Robotics (first commercial deployment, Digit in logistics), and Leju Robot (乐聚机器人) (~5% market share, partnered with Huawei Cloud).
