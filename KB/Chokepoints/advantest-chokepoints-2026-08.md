## Probe cards - device-specific design lock and HBM/leading-edge qualification

`A-C1` · **Tier 2** · Severity **Medium** · Evidence **INFERRED** — no named source — analyst inference

**Named suppliers:** FormFactor (~28.5% 2025), Technoprobe (~16.5% 2025, from ~8% in 2018), Micronics Japan / MJC (~10.2% 2025)

**Non-listed / low-disclosure:** Technoprobe and several Japanese/Korean probe specialists have thin analyst coverage relative to their criticality

**Concentration:** REFRAMED 31 Jul 2026: this is moderate concentration, not a supply chokepoint. Top three ~55-60% of 2025 revenue, top five ~73%. NOTE an inconsistency in the source: the three named shares sum to 55.2% while the same report states 'roughly 60%'. The real lock is per-device: probe cards are designed to a specific device, so switching is a re-design not a re-order. MJC was qualified by TSMC as a primary 2nm supplier after ~18 months of co-development

**Substitutability and lead time:** Low at the device level, high at the market level. Technoprobe opened an EUR80m Dresden plant in 2025; FormFactor acquired a California MEMS firm for $120m. Capacity is being added

**Geographic concentration:** USA, Italy, Japan

**Watch for:** Sole-qualification of any single probe-card supplier for HBM4 or 2nm at a top-three memory maker or foundry

**Basis / source:** StellarMR / Mordor / Valuates probe-card and MEMS probe-card reports 2025-26 (secondary, and internally inconsistent on the top-three total - noted above). Severity lowered because ~60% across three suppliers with active capacity addition does not support a High rating
