## Underfill for HBM stacking - MR-MUF (SK Hynix) and TC-NCF (Samsung)

`SH-C3` · **Tier 3** · Severity **Critical** · Evidence **INFERRED** — no named source — analyst inference

**Named suppliers:** Namics (MR-MUF underfill, reportedly exclusive to SK Hynix), Resonac (ex-Showa Denko/Hitachi Chemical), Henkel

**Non-listed / low-disclosure:** Namics Corporation - NON-LISTED Japanese specialty materials maker, Niigata. Reported exclusive supplier of SK Hynix's MR-MUF underfill. Thinly covered relative to criticality - highest-value primary-sourcing target on this map

**Concentration:** REFRAMED 31 Jul 2026: SK Hynix does not principally use TC-NCF - it uses proprietary MR-MUF with Namics underfill, reported as exclusive. So SK Hynix's exposure is to a SINGLE non-listed Japanese supplier, which is sharper than the previous 'NCF materials' framing. Across NCF generally, Resonac + Henkel + Namics hold ~99%

**Substitutability and lead time:** Very low near term - material change is a full stack requalification. BUT hybrid bonding is the structural escape route: SK Hynix completed 12-high HBM hybrid-bonding validation as of Apr 2026, which removes the underfill layer entirely

**Geographic concentration:** Japan

**Watch for:** Any SK Hynix second-source qualification for MR-MUF underfill; hybrid bonding entering volume for HBM4E/HBM5

**Basis / source:** nomadsemi 'Deep Dive on HBM' (MR-MUF / Namics exclusivity, Samsung TC-NCF split); QYResearch + Valuates NCF-for-HBM share data 2025; hybrid-bonding validation reported Apr 2026. Namics exclusivity is the load-bearing claim and is trade-press sourced only - VERIFY BEFORE PUBLISHING
