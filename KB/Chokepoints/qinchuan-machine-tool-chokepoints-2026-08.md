# Qinchuan Machine Tool (秦川机床) — upstream chokepoint map

**Ticker:** 000837.SZ · **Sector:** Humanoid Robotics & Physical AI · **Sub-segment:** Gear Machine Tools + Reducers · **Jurisdiction:** China

**Supply chain role:** Gear grinding machine maker AND RV/harmonic reducer maker

**Chokepoint hypothesis:** Vertically integrated across two layers of this map - it makes both the machine that grinds the gear and the reducer itself. Broke the Klingelnberg / Mitsubishi Heavy monopoly at the machine layer. Its own residual dependency is the CNC control and measurement layer.

5 mapped chokepoints. Tier 1 sits directly upstream of Qinchuan Machine Tool (秦川机床); Tier 2 and Tier 3 sit above that.

## High-end CNC control systems

`QM-C1` · **Tier 1** · Severity **Critical** · Evidence **INFERRED** — no named source — analyst inference

**Named suppliers:** FANUC, Siemens SINUMERIK, Mitsubishi, Heidenhain (foreign); Huazhong CNC 300161.SZ, Kede CNC (domestic)

**Non-listed / low-disclosure:** The four foreign incumbents are the constraint. Heidenhain is NON-LISTED.

**Concentration:** Four firms hold 75-80% of the global high-end CNC market, up to 90%+ including white-label. China's high-end CNC domestic rate is ~6% - the lowest single link in the machine tool chain and the acknowledged number-one 卡脖子 point.

**Substitutability and lead time:** Very low at the high end. Domestic share forecast to reach only ~19% by 2029 from ~8% in 2022.

**Geographic concentration:** Japan, Germany

**Watch for:** Huazhong CNC or Kede CNC winning a high-end 5-axis design-in at a major gear machine maker.

**Basis / source:** Chinese securities research and industry reports, Jul 2026. 2022 domestic rates: high 7.9% / mid 28.3% / low 75.3%.

## Zerodur glass-ceramic scale substrate

`QM-C5` · **Tier 3** · Severity **High** · Evidence **INFERRED** — no named source — analyst inference

**Named suppliers:** Schott AG (Zerodur); Corning (ULE, competing material)

**Non-listed / low-disclosure:** Schott AG - NON-LISTED, part of the CARL ZEISS FOUNDATION. Same foundation that owns Carl Zeiss SMT, ASML's sole EUV optics supplier.

**Concentration:** Heidenhain's linear scales use Zerodur glass ceramic, a Schott material. Zerodur production runs in two stages - melting then ceramization - each taking several months per blank depending on size.

**Substitutability and lead time:** Very low, and slow by physics. Multi-month production per blank is a hard throughput floor, not a capex problem.

**Geographic concentration:** Germany

**Watch for:** Schott Zerodur capacity or lead-time disclosure; any allocation shift between astronomy, lithography and metrology demand.

**Basis / source:** Heidenhain product documentation confirms Zerodur scales; Zerodur is a Schott trademark; Schott is part of the Carl Zeiss Foundation.
