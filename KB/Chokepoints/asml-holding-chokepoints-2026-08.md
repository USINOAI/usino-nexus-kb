## Collector mirror / pellicle

`ASML-C4` · **Tier 3** · Severity **Critical** · Evidence **INFERRED** — no named source — analyst inference

**Named suppliers:** Pellicle: Mitsui Chemicals (sole commercial manufacturer, under ASML licence and to ASML design, Iwakuni-Ohtake Works). Collector mirror: Carl Zeiss SMT; Media Lario (status unverified)

**Non-listed / low-disclosure:** Media Lario S.r.l. - NON-LISTED Italian optics specialist, historically a collector-mirror supplier. CURRENT status still unverified as of 31 Jul 2026 - do not present as a live supplier

**Concentration:** Pellicle: single commercial source. ASML-Mitsui licence signed 31 May 2019; Mitsui commenced commercial production 26 May 2021 as the world's first EUV pellicle manufacturer, taking over assembly and production from ASML itself. Collector: Zeiss, no known alternative

**Substitutability and lead time:** Very low. Collector is a consumable with real economics: a ~1.2nm tin layer costs ~20% collector efficiency and ~10% tool throughput; replacement roughly annually or every ~3,000 exposure hours. Next-generation CNT pellicles (imec-Mitsui partnership, announced Dec 2023, targeted 2025-26) are needed for higher-power sources

**Geographic concentration:** Germany, Italy

**Watch for:** Mitsui pellicle capacity or transmission/yield disclosure; CNT pellicle qualification slip; collector replacement-interval change

**Basis / source:** PRIMARY: Mitsui Chemicals news releases 31 May 2019 (ASML licence) and 26 May 2021 (commercial production). Optica OPN Dec 2023 on imec-Mitsui CNT pellicle. Collector lifetime figures from Asianometry - secondary, not ASML-stated
