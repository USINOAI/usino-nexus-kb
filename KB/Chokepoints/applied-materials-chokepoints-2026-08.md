## High-vacuum valves & vacuum components

`AMAT-C1` · **Tier 2** · Severity **High** · Evidence **ACTUAL** — primary source (filing, company release, or regulator text)

**Named suppliers:** VAT Group (~70% of its core semiconductor vacuum-valve applications), MKS Instruments, ULVAC, Pfeiffer Vacuum, Agilent; Ichor and Ferrotec at sub-assembly level

**Concentration:** VAT ~70% share in core semiconductor applications; >34% of the broader vacuum-valve market in 2025. Top five (VAT, MKS, ULVAC, Pfeiffer, Agilent) ~55% of the broad market

**Substitutability and lead time:** Low. Qualified vacuum components are slow to re-source. Vacuum-valve market forecast $3.4bn 2026 to $8.1bn 2035, so capacity is being added into a rising WFE cycle

**Geographic concentration:** Switzerland, USA, Japan

**Watch for:** VAT order intake and lead times in quarterly results; MKS or Ichor share-gain disclosure

**Basis / source:** VAT Group investor relations positioning, repeated in Morningstar equity research 2025-26 ('dominant vacuum franchise', ~70% core semi share); GM Insights vacuum-valve market 2025 for the broad-market split. CAVEAT: the ~70% is company-stated and analyst-repeated, not independently measured
