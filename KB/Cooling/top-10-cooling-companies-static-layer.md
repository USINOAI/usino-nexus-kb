**Company History & Founding Story**  
Vertiv's history is a story of consolidation in the critical infrastructure space. It was formerly part of Emerson Electric's Network Power business. In 2016, a consortium led by Platinum Equity acquired this business, and it was rebranded as Vertiv. The company went public in 2020. It has since grown aggressively, making strategic acquisitions like **ThermoKey**, **E&I Engineering**, and **Geist** to expand its thermal management and liquid cooling capabilities . Today, Vertiv is recognized as a market leader in precision cooling for data centers, holding a **23% global market share** .  
  
**Core Technology & IP**  
Vertiv's core technology is its **Liebert** brand of precision cooling equipment, which includes air conditioning, chillers, and liquid cooling systems. A key part of its modern IP is its **XDU** line of Coolant Distribution Units (CDUs), which are critical for circulating coolant in high-density AI environments and can support racks exceeding 200kW . They are investing heavily in testing large-scale chillers integrated with liquid cooling systems to validate performance under extreme AI workloads .  
  
**Business Model**  
Vertiv operates as a pure-play critical infrastructure provider for data centers and communication networks. Its business model involves selling hardware (chillers, racks, power distribution), software, and services (installation, maintenance, and lifecycle support) . The company is a specialist in thermal management, which is its primary focus, making it a key go-to partner for AI infrastructure build-outs .  
  
**Position in the Supply Chain**  
- **Upstream (Suppliers):** Vertiv relies on a global network of component suppliers for compressors, motors, and electronic components. It is scaling its own manufacturing, notably expanding its facility near Padua, Italy, to double chiller production capacity .  
- **Downstream (Customers):** Its customers are the world's largest data center operators, cloud providers, and enterprises. It is a key partner for companies building AI-ready infrastructure, with a massive addressable market as data center capacity is projected to grow by 32.9 GW by 2027 .  
  
**Key Leadership Bios**  
- **Gio Albertazzi (CEO):** Appointed CEO in 2024, he has been instrumental in driving Vertiv's strategic focus on AI and high-performance computing. He has emphasized the company's ability to meet the unprecedented thermal demands of new silicon and high-density AI workloads .  
  
**Controversies / Strategic Pivots**  
Vertiv's main "controversy" is the competitive pressure in the AI infrastructure space. It faces stiff competition from **Schneider Electric** and **Super Micro Computer**, which are also expanding their AI-focused cooling offerings . Its strategic pivot is a complete embrace of liquid cooling as the new standard, moving beyond its legacy as a primarily air-cooling specialist. The company views thermal management as a critical system, not just a component, a philosophy it is embedding into its service and product design .
