## Advanced Packaging – Top 5 Companies  
The advanced packaging market reached ~$46B in 2024 and is projected to exceed $120B by 2031. Here are the dominant players:  
## 1. TSMC (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company)  
* **Position**: #1 in interposer and fan-out wafer-level packaging with ~60% market share. CoWoS (Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate) is the critical bottleneck for AI accelerator packaging.  
* **Why It Matters**: TSMC's CoWoS capacity constraints directly impact NVIDIA, AMD, and every hyperscaler's ability to ship AI chips. They are aggressively scaling CoWoS and SoIC (System-on-Integrated-Chips) capacity.  
* **2024 Advanced Packaging Revenue**: ~$5.08B  
## 2. ASE Group (with SPIL)  
* **Position**: Leading OSAT (Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test) provider. ASE together with SPIL is the largest OSAT player globally.  
* **Why It Matters**: ASE is the primary non-TSMC packaging alternative. They are expanding U.S. capacity under CHIPS Act incentives to serve NVIDIA and Apple.  
* **2024 Advanced Packaging Revenue**: ~$5.38B (combined with SPIL)  
## 3. Amkor Technology  
* **Position**: #2 OSAT globally, major U.S.-based packaging provider.  
* **Why It Matters**: Amkor is a key strategic alternative to Asian packaging dominance. They are also expanding U.S. capacity under CHIPS Act incentives.  
* **2024 Advanced Packaging Revenue**: ~$5.38B  
## 4. JCET (Jiangsu Changjiang Electronics Technology)  
* **Position**: China's largest advanced packaging player.  
* **Why It Matters**: JCET is central to China's push for packaging self-sufficiency. They announced a $1.5B investment to strengthen domestic advanced packaging capabilities. Critical for Chinese AI chip players (Huawei, Alibaba) who cannot rely on TSMC.  
* **2024 Advanced Packaging Revenue**: ~$2.78B  
## 5. Samsung Electronics  
* **Position**: Major IDM (Integrated Device Manufacturer) with I-Cube packaging platform using hybrid copper bonding for sub-20μm pitch integration.  
* **Why It Matters**: Samsung is TSMC's only real foundry competitor and is investing heavily in advanced packaging to support its own memory and logic businesses.  
* **2024 Advanced Packaging Revenue**: ~$3.18B  
**Honorable Mentions**: Intel ($6.58B in advanced packaging revenue, leading in EMIB and 3D stacking), SK Hynix (critical HBM supplier), and Tongfu Microelectronics (investing ~$1B in advanced packaging).
