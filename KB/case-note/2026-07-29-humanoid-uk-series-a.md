# CASE NOTE #4 — 2026-07-29
- Event: London-based Humanoid closed a $152M (€133M) Series A on 2026-07-21 at $1.35bn post-money — Europe's first pure-play humanoid unicorn, ~2 years after founding, $270M raised total. Led by Prime Movers Lab with Schaeffler, Bosch, Fubon Financial VC (Taiwan) and Aglaé Ventures (Arnault). Product is HMND 01 Alpha — a *wheeled* mobile manipulator, not a biped — running proprietary KinetIQ VLA software; wheels chosen explicitly for faster European certification. Claims 34,000 pre-orders / $2.4bn pipeline with zero commercial units delivered. Schaeffler (binding agreement, 2026-05-13) supplies >half of joint actuators through 2031 *and* takes a four-digit fleet across its own plants by 2032; Bosch is manufacturing partner with capacity reserved for 100,000 units over five years. First beta deployments in logistics/manufacturing/retail Q4 2026.

Schaeffler guides its humanoid unit to a "hundreds of millions of euros" order book by 2030. Peer set: Figure ~$39bn, 1X ~$10bn, Apptronik ~$5.5bn, Unitree ~$1.3bn.
- What everyone misses: Bosch and Schaeffler are wearing four hats at once — investor, component supplier, contract manufacturer, and customer. The $2.4bn "pipeline" and the Schaeffler offtake are therefore not independent demand validation; they're a vendor-financed loop where the counterparties validating the valuation have a structural interest in it. What the Germans are actually buying is an option on not being disintermediated, and their real economics are actuator content per unit, not the equity. Second thing nobody says out loud: Europe's flagship humanoid company just conceded that legs don't work commercially — HMND 01 is an AMR with arms, competing in a category where Locus, Geek+ and every Chinese AMR maker already fight on price. The moat claim rests entirely on KinetIQ, not the form factor.
- My read: Don't chase the OEM narrative layer — chase the actuator layer, which gets paid whether or not Humanoid ships a single unit. This is the third consecutive data point for the same thesis in this book (Note #1 Mirle/Kedali reducers, Note #2 Dobot VLA, now this): value accrues to components and software, not to humanoid brands. Cleanest listed proxy is Schaeffler (SHA0.DE) @ €7.84 (corrected 2026-07-30; see Market reaction) — you get the actuator contract, the equity stake and the customer deployment at an auto-supplier multiple, i.e. the robotics option is roughly free. Secondary: Harmonic Drive (6324.T), Nabtesco (6268.T), Mirle (2464.TW). Valuation arbitrage worth flagging: $1.35bn for zero delivered units vs Unitree at ~$1.3bn having shipped 5,500+ in 2025 — Western humanoids are priced on narrative, Chinese on units.
