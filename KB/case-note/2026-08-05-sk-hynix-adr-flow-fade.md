# CASE NOTE #9 — 2026-08-05
- Event: CXMT (state-linked Chinese DRAM maker) debuted on the Shanghai exchange 07-27, closing +466% day one, raising $8.6bn at a ~$488bn market cap — the same week Shanghai Aishengna's first domestic immersion DUV tools began shipping to SMIC, Hua Hong and CXMT (Note #3's catalyst). - What everyone misses: Tuesday's crash had two separable legs, and the bounce is un-separating them. One is a capex-sentiment misread — Note #6 already argues SK Hynix's own capex number was punished twice, at itself and at KLA, and has a real case to partly reverse. The other is structural: CXMT is now a real, $488bn-cap, $8.6bn-flush domestic competitor sitting on top of domestic DUV tools that are actually shipping, not promised — and nothing about that changed this week. An Iran shipping deal and a Palantir beat say nothing about CXMT's balance sheet or Aishengna's tool count. On top of that, SK Hynix outran Samsung by 2–3 points the same week its brand-new ADR got its first-ever coverage wave and a specific MSCI flow estimate — a mechanical, one-time bump dressed up as fresh conviction.
- My read: Fade SK Hynix's relative outperformance over Samsung — the extra points it gained 08-05 look like ADR-coverage and index-flow noise stacked on a shared macro bounce, not differential fundamentals, and neither name has actually re-priced the CXMT/domestic-DUV leg that did most of the 07-28 damage. Call: short/underweight **000660.KS @ ₩1,689,000 (Wed 2026-08-05 KRX close)**, expecting it to give back Wednesday's gap over 005930.KS. Runs parallel to Note #6, not against it — if #6's capex-misread thesis plays out, that's a legitimate reason for both stocks to recover, which would weaken this fade's relative-outperformance logic specifically. Sits directly on Note #3's structural leg: strengthens if Aishengna's ~20-unit 2027 ramp holds, weakens if #3's optics-chokepoint update (fragile ramp) is right.
