My read: Mirle's run already prices the Tesla news; the underpriced angle is Kedali and the Thailand corridor. Watch whether other Chinese precision-parts firms announce ASEAN JVs in the next 2 quarters — that's the confirming signal.
Watch: Rayong plant construction/production start dates; Mirle Q3 earnings (Nov 2026); any second China-Taiwan-ASEAN robot parts JV announcement; Optimus production ramp guidance.
