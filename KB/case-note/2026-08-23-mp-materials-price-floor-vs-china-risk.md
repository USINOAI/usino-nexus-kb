# CASE NOTE #11 — 2026-08-23
- Event: MP Materials (MP) ran from $38.00 (Fri 2026-07-29 close) to $60.05 (Fri 2026-08-21 close), +58% in three weeks. Aug 6 2026 Q2 print showed revenue +89% YoY to $108.5M and Adjusted EBITDA turning positive to $28.5M — but that figure is carried almost entirely by the DoD's Price Protection Agreement, which sets a $110/kg NdPr floor through 2035; without it, Adjusted EBITDA would have been $10.9M, not $28.5M. Friday 08-21's 9% single-day pop coincided with the DOE naming seven critical-minerals projects for $500M in funding — lithium extraction, cobalt refining, battery recycling. MP was not one of the seven.
- Market reaction: rare-earth and critical-minerals names rallied together Friday on the DOE headline; MP led the group higher despite having no direct stake in that specific announcement.
- What everyone misses: the rally is being read as a China-rare-earth-risk trade, but MP's own Q2 numbers say most of its newly-profitable economics are a government-guaranteed floor locked through 2035, not open-market pricing power. Friday's specific catalyst wasn't even about MP or magnets — it was sector sympathy. That makes MP a weaker, not stronger, read on the Nov 10/11 2026 China resumption date (ALERT_SPEC_rare_earth.md's highest-information day) than the market is currently treating it as, because MP's price floor insulates it from whatever Beijing decides either direction.
- My read: don't use MP as the proxy for a Nov 10/11 China-escalation trade — its DoD floor mutes the read in both directions. For direct exposure to that date, the Dy/Tb oxide series itself or a name without a price floor (e.g. Lynas) is the cleaner instrument. MP @ $60.05 (Fri 2026-08-21 close, verified).
